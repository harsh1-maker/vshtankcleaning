Our process

What VSH Tank Cleaning includes

Every booking follows a proper cleaning process from draining to final handover.

💧 1

Drain the tank

Remaining water is pumped out so the floor and walls are exposed.

▱ 2

Remove the sludge

Settled mud, silt and slime are removed before scrubbing.

✦ 3

Pressure-wash & scrub

Walls, floor, corners and inlet areas are cleaned thoroughly.

✓ 4

Disinfect

The cleaned surfaces are disinfected before the tank is ready to refill.

Photo proof
