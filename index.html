FAQs

Frequently asked questions

How much does tank cleaning cost? +

Tank cleaning starts from ₹499 for an overhead tank up to 500 litres. The exact price depends on tank type and capacity.

Do you offer discounts for multiple tanks? +

Yes. Quantity discounts are available: 2 tanks = 10%, 3 = 12%, 4 = 14% and 5 or more = 15%.

Is there an additional online payment discount? +

Yes. Full online payment gets an additional 5% discount after the quantity discount.

Do you provide service outside Delhi? +

We currently focus on Delhi NCR and selected nearby areas. Check the Service Areas page or contact us on WhatsApp to confirm your location.

Can I pay after the service? +

Yes. Pay-after-service is available. The additional 5% online payment discount does not apply to pay-after-service bookings.
