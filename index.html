How it works

What happens when VSH cleans your tank.

01

Drain

Remaining water is removed so the tank floor and walls are exposed.

02

Remove sludge

Settled mud, silt and slime are physically removed.

03

Scrub & wash

Walls, corners and surfaces are scrubbed and pressure washed.

04

Disinfect

Cleaned surfaces are disinfected and rinsed before refilling.

05

Photo proof

You receive before-and-after proof of the completed work.
