HOW WE CLEAN

Our Water Tank Cleaning Process

A systematic multi-step cleaning process for a cleaner and safer tank.

01
🧹

Surroundings Cleaning

We clean the manhole and surrounding area before starting the tank cleaning process.

02
💧

Dewatering

Existing water is removed from the tank so the tank can be properly cleaned.

03
🪣

Sludge Removal

Dirt, sludge and contaminated water are removed from the tank.

04
🧽

Scrubbing

Walls and surfaces are manually scrubbed to remove dirt, fungus and stains.

05
💦

High Pressure Cleaning

High-pressure water jets are used to thoroughly clean the tank surfaces.

06
🧹

Vacuum Cleaning

Remaining water and residue are removed for a cleaner tank interior.

07
🛡️

Anti-Bacterial Spray

Anti-bacterial treatment is applied to the internal surfaces.

08
✨

UV Treatment

Final treatment helps provide an additional level of hygiene.
