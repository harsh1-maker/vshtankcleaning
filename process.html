OUR PROCESS

A proper tank clean is more than a rinse.

VSH follows a structured cleaning process designed to remove sludge, dirt, algae and buildup instead of simply mixing it back into the water.

01

Drain the tank

The remaining water is removed so that the floor and walls are properly exposed before cleaning begins.

Water removal
02

Remove sludge & sediment

Settled mud, silt and sludge are removed from the bottom rather than simply being stirred into suspension.

Deep cleaning
03

Scrub & pressure wash

The tank floor, walls, corners, lid and inlet areas are scrubbed and pressure-washed to loosen stubborn buildup.

Mechanical cleaning
04

Disinfect

After physical cleaning, the surfaces are treated with an appropriate disinfecting solution and rinsed properly.

Hygiene step
05

Photo proof & handover

Before-and-after photos can be shared with the customer. The tank is checked and properly closed before handover.

Proof of service
