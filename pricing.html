Frequently asked

Questions before booking

Do you clean overhead tanks?

Yes. VSH provides cleaning for overhead, rooftop and loft water tanks.

Do you clean underground tanks?

Yes. Underground sump and water storage tanks can also be booked.

How does the quantity discount work?

The discount is applied to the combined service subtotal when multiple tanks are booked together.

Is the 5% discount available with pay after service?

No. The additional 5% discount is available only when you choose online payment.

Can I pay after the service?

Yes. Select Pay After Service during booking. No online payment is required for that option.

Which areas are currently supported?

VSH currently supports Delhi NCR PIN codes beginning with 110, 121, 122 and 201.
