ABOUT VSH

Professional cleaning for the tanks behind your water supply.

VSH Tank Cleaning provides professional overhead and underground water tank cleaning services for homes, societies, offices, shops and commercial properties across Delhi NCR.

Why VSH exists

Water tanks are often forgotten because they stay out of sight. But the condition of the tank directly affects the water stored inside it. Dust, sediment, sludge, algae and buildup can collect over time.

Our goal is simple: make professional tank cleaning straightforward, transparent and easy to book. Customers should know what they are paying for, what the cleaning includes and when the work will be done.
